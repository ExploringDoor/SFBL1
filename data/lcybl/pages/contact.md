The league is run entirely by volunteers. Please use the contact route that matches your question. Individual email addresses and phone numbers are not published on this site.

## Schedule and score corrections

**Webmaster.** Arbiter is the master schedule. The website is downstream of it. If the website does not match Arbiter, send the age, section, game number and a description of the error. Corrections normally appear within about 24 hours.

Coaches can submit results and reschedule requests using the forms on the Schedule / Scores page. Only the Head Coach should submit a reschedule request, and the opposing coach must agree to the new date.

## Scheduling and Arbiter questions

**League President.** Coaches with Arbiter or scheduling questions, including umpire cancellations due to weather before game day, should contact the League President.

## League business, membership and rules

**League President** and the **Executive Committee**. Boundary Rule requests, Double Roster Rule questions, roster and registration matters, and general league business.

## Club tournaments

Tournament entry questions go to the hosting club. Contact the league and your enquiry will be passed to the tournament director.

## Meetings

Meetings are held at **Penn Medicine Park, 650 N Prince St, Lancaster, PA 17603**. Meeting dates are published in the League Documents section.
