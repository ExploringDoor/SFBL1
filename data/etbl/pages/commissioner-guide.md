A one-page guide for the seven town commissioners. It is not linked from the menu — the league shares this address with commissioners directly. Your town's password comes from the league separately; never post it here.

## Signing in

1. Open the site and choose **More → Admin**, or go straight to `/admin`.
2. Type **your town's password**. You land on the **Scores** tab with only your town's games listed, and the badge in the corner names your town.
3. Done for the day? Tap **Sign out**.

You cannot see or change another town's games, the schedule, the teams or anything else in the admin — that is on purpose, so nobody has to worry about an accidental click.

## Entering scores (after every game)

1. On the **Scores** tab, find the game. The default view is *Needs score*; switch to *All games* to correct a score you already entered.
2. Type the away score and the home score, then press **Enter** (or tap **Save**).
3. The scoreboard, the standings and the team pages update on their own within a minute.

Only games with a team from your town appear. If a game you expect is missing, the league admin may not have set that team's **Town** yet — ask them to check the Teams tab.

If both towns enter the same game, the last save wins. Talk to the other commissioner before changing a score they entered.

## Volunteers (clock, scorebook, snack bar)

The **Volunteers** tab manages the sign-up board parents see at `/volunteers`.

- **Generate from the schedule** creates one shift per game per job for a date range — usually the coming Saturday or two. Tick the jobs you need and how many people each needs. Running it again after a schedule change is safe: existing shifts keep their sign-ups.
- **Edit / Delete / + Add shift** for one-offs.
- **Who signed up** shows names, emails and phones for the league only. Parents' contact details never appear on the website — the board shows a first name and last initial.
- Send families to **`/volunteers`**. No account needed; they leave a name and a way to reach them.

## Something's wrong?

Contact the league (see the [Contact](/content/contact) page). Every score entry is logged, so a mistaken save is easy to trace and fix.
