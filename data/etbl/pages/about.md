The **East Texas Basketball League** is developmental youth basketball for 3rd through 6th graders across seven East Texas towns, based in Mineola.

## How the league works

- **One commissioner per town.** Each town runs its own program — Mineola's is the Mineola Little Dribblers — and sends one representative to the league. There is no central office; the commissioners run it together.
- **Six divisions.** Teams are grouped by grade and by boys / girls so kids play kids their own age.
- **Saturday games in local gyms.** Every town hosts. Find every gym, with directions, on the [Gyms](/fields) page.
- **Scores and standings, every week.** Commissioners post scores after the games; standings update on their own.

## What it is for

The sole purpose of this league is to get kids ready for middle-school basketball. It is not a league for coaches to chase rings. Equal playing time, fundamentals, and sportsmanship come before the scoreboard.

## Get involved

Every game needs a clock, a scorebook and a snack bar. Parents can [sign up for a game-day job](/volunteers) in about a minute — no account needed.
