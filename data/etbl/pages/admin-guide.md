For whoever holds the **league admin password** (not the town passwords — those are covered in the [Commissioner Guide](/content/commissioner-guide)). This page is not in the menu.

## Signing in

**More → Admin**, type the league password. You get every tab. Tap **Sign out** when you're done on a shared computer.

## The tabs you'll actually use

- **Scores** — every game in the league. The **All towns** dropdown narrows it to one town; commissioners see only their own.
- **Schedule** — add, move or cancel games, mark a date rained/snowed out, fix a time or gym. Every change is logged.
- **Build Schedule** — generates a whole division's season at once. See *Building the season schedule* below.
- **Teams** — each team's name, division and **Town**. The Town must be spelled the same on every team from that town; it is what decides which games a commissioner may score. Deactivate a team that drops out rather than deleting it.
- **Gyms** — the gym list with addresses. This feeds the Gyms page, the schedule editor's gym dropdown and the schedule builder.
- **Volunteers** — generate clock / scorebook / snack-bar shifts from the schedule, edit them, and see who signed up (contact details never appear on the website).
- **Playoffs** — build the end-of-season bracket: divisions, rounds, matchups, then results. The public **Playoffs** page (under More) says "not published yet" until you tick *Active*.
- **Alerts** — the banner across the top of the home page: weather, closures, deadlines. Clear it when it's over.
- **Send Message** — email or text everyone who signed up for alerts.
- **Rules** and **Pages** — edit the rulebook and the About / Contact / Sponsors pages in place.
- **Sponsors** — logos for the footer strip on every page.
- **Health** — a snapshot of the league, plus **Remove sample season** (see below).

## Building the season schedule

**Build Schedule** makes a division's whole season in one go and shows it to you before anything is saved. Do one division at a time; each run works around the games already on the calendar, so two divisions never get the same gym at the same time.

1. **Teams** — pick the division. Everyone in it is ticked; untick any team that isn't playing.
2. **When** — first Saturday, last Saturday, and the off days (Thanksgiving weekend, the Christmas break). Leave *Games for each team* on *Everyone plays everyone* unless the league wants a fixed number of games instead.
3. **Gyms and times** — add every gym that's available and tap the start times it runs. A gym with two courts: set *Courts* to 2.
4. **Teams that must not play each other** — only if there are any.
5. **Game rules** — the settings that make a game day sane. The ones that matter here: *Season shape* (everyone plays everyone once), *Game length* 75 minutes, *Games per team per day* 1, and **Teams that share a coach** — click the two teams a coach runs (a 3rd-grade and a 5th-grade team, say) and they'll never be in two gyms at the same moment. Press *Save these rules* once; they're kept for next time.
6. Press **Build schedule**. The preview lists every game by week, with a **grade** (home/away balance, fair share of early and late starts, spread across gyms). *Try another layout* gives you a different draw with the same settings — keep the one you like. The **both towns could host** list is where you decide who's home when two towns each have a gym.
7. **Create the games** puts them on the live schedule. If you change your mind straight away, *Undo this generation* takes back exactly those games. *Save as draft* keeps a version without posting it.

Two cautions. Leave *Club / organization* in *Per-team settings* **empty** — it means "these teams never play each other", which is not what a town is. And build every division before you hand-edit any game, so the builder sees the full picture.

## Sample season

Until the real teams are loaded, the site shows placeholder teams, gyms and results with a yellow "Sample data" banner. When the real data goes in, **Health → Remove sample season** deletes every sample team, game, result and generated volunteer shift in one go and takes the banner down. It never touches anything that wasn't sample data.

## What goes through the league's site builder

Loading the real teams, schedule and gyms from your spreadsheets, changing the logo or colours, and anything about the domain or sign-in passwords. Send those over rather than retyping them into the admin — they load in one step and stay consistent.
