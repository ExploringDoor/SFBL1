## Common questions

**I want to get on a team.** Contact the president of the division you want to play
in, or post in the league's player pick-up group on Facebook.

**My team needs to register.** Team registration is held in person each spring at
American Legion Post 2. See League Info for the details.

**A game got rained out.** Makeup schedules are posted by the league during the
season. Check the schedule page, which is updated as makeups are set.

**Roster change after submitting.** Use the change of roster form on the Forms page.

**Umpire question or evaluation.** Contact the Helena District Umpire In Chief.
