---
Two kinds of tournament run alongside the LCYBL season: club-hosted tournaments, which member clubs organise and open to LCYBL teams, and the league's own LNP Tournament and year-end tournaments.

Tournament entry questions go to the hosting club. Contact the tournament director via the league. See the Contact page.

## Section Two Playoffs

## 2026 12U Section Two Playoffs and 2026 14U Section Two Playoffs

Both sections use the same format:

- #D1 is the Division Winner with the best record
- #D2 is the Division Winner with the next best record
- Seeds #3 to #8 are the remaining teams with the best records
- Single elimination
- Tiebreakers are: Points, Head to head (calculated end of season), Runs allowed, Coin flip
- Two umpires are provided and paid by LCYBL at a later time
- Home team must supply balls
- Pitch count carries over from the regular season
- Rain date is the next day. If the game is stopped due to weather, the game will continue the next day
- Pitch counts are the same as the regular season. Example: a pitcher has 26 pitches, he will not be eligible to pitch the next day
- The winner submits the score to the league website
- Teams with a winning percentage of .750 or above may be ineligible

## 2026 10U Section Two Year End Tournament

- June 26 to 28, 2026
- Host club: Mountville
- 24 team single elimination tournament. If some teams opt out or do not qualify the bracket will change
- Games start on Friday. No rescheduling
- Seeding is determined by league record
- All 10u Section 2 teams qualify unless they are one of the .750 teams
- 1 hour 45 minute limit or 6 innings
- Home team is the higher seeded team
- Tiebreakers are: Points, Head to head (calculated end of season), Runs allowed, Coin flip
- Normal pitch count rules are in effect (daily total). Pitch count rules continue from the regular season
- 2 umpires are paid by LCYBL at a later time
- Teams must supply balls
- No pre-game warmups on the infield
- Rain date is the next day
- Head coaches must confirm with the tournament director that their team will be playing

Tournament instructions:

- Each team must check in at the concession stand upon arrival of the first game with new game balls
- No pregame warm up on the infield at any time during the weekend. All pregame activity must be done in the outfield. You may use a pop up for soft toss into a net. No BP in the outfield outside of soft toss into a net
- No use of the T ball field for team BP, infield, or pitcher warmup at any time
- Player pitch count sheets must be presented amongst coaches before each game. Days of rest in effect for the tournament. If a pitcher is available he gets a maximum of 65 pitches for the day. It can be over multiple games. Pitch count restrictions from the week carry into the weekend

**Field locations:** Froelich Park, 450 W Main St, Mountville, PA 17554. Mountville Borough Park, 158 Clay St, Mountville, PA 17554. Veteran's Memorial Park, 40 Spruce Rd, Mountville, PA 17554.

**Teams participating:** Mountville, Penn Manor Gold, Manheim Township Thunder, Manheim Township Lightning, Warwick Rangers, St Leo, Hempfield Grey, Cedar Crest, Penn Manor Blue, L-S Regulators, Lanc Junior Tornados, Manheim VFW Gray, Ephrata Athletics, Warwick Diamondbacks, Hempfield Red, Penn Manor White, Cocalico, Donegal Green, L-S Blue Crew, Hempfield Black, Hempfield White, Solanco Black, CV, Columbia.
