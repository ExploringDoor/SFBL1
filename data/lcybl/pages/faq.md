## Frequently Asked Questions

## My schedule is not correct between the website and Arbiter

Arbiter is the master. Contact the Webmaster with the Age, Section, Game number and the correction. See the Contact page.

## How do I call in scores into LNP?

*The answer published on the league's own site is incomplete and has not been carried over. Please contact the league for the current score reporting number. See the Contact page.*

## How do I reschedule a game or rain out?

Contact the league. See the Contact page. Coaches can also use the Reschedule Game Request form on the Schedule / Scores page. Only the Head Coach should submit a reschedule request, and the opposing coach must agree to the new date.

We try to update them after the scores are updated in the paper.

Usually, within 24 hours.

## When does fall ball start?

The weekend after Labor Day.

## When does fall ball end?

2nd Weekend in October but can play after that weekend if both coaches agree.

*Note: the Fall Ball information sheet states that the end of season is the 3rd weekend in October. The league should confirm which is correct.*
