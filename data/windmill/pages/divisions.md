# Divisions & Skill Levels

Windmill Fastpitch groups teams two ways: by **age division** for play and standings, and by **skill level** so games stay competitive. Teams are placed regionally so families spend less time driving.

## Age divisions

### 8U Machine Pitch
Wednesday nights, single games at 5:30 PM. **No scores are kept** — this level is about learning the game. Tournament seeding is a blind draw.

### 8U Live Pitch
Wednesday nights, single games at 6:00 PM. Games run **90 minutes or 6 innings**, whichever comes first. Eight weeks of play.

### 10U
Monday nights, **doubleheaders** at 6:00 and 7:30 PM. Scores and standings are kept. 10U picks up its season the second week and makes up the opening Monday later in the schedule.

### 12U
Tuesday nights, **doubleheaders** at 5:45 and 7:15 PM. Scores and standings kept.

### 14U
Thursday nights, **doubleheaders** at 5:45 and 7:15 PM. Scores and standings kept.

### High School
Wednesday nights at 7:00 PM. Single 7-inning games, no time limit. A shorter **six-week** season that begins after the state tournament.

## Skill levels

Every team also chooses a level of competition so games are matched fairly. Teams that are not fully recreational typically enter at D2 or D3.

- **D1 · Competitive** — the most experienced, competitive teams
- **D2 · Upper Intermediate** — strong teams a step below competitive
- **D3 · Intermediate** — recreational play; the middle tier and the most common
- **D4 · Developmental** — newer teams and mostly first-year players
