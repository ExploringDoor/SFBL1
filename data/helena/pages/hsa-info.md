# About the Helena Softball Association

The Helena Softball Association is the home of adult slowpitch softball for Lewis &
Clark, Jefferson, and Broadwater Counties. Games are played Monday through Friday
nights at the Batch and Centennial field complexes in Helena.

HSA is a volunteer-run 501(c)(3) organization affiliated with USA Softball. Through
that affiliation the league provides training, background checks, SafeSport training,
coaching clinics, player, coach and officiating development, and sanctioned
competition.

> The purposes of the HSA are to cause, foster, and enhance youth/adult sports
> activities in the community. As volunteers the organization coordinates and
> cooperates with all organizations, agencies, activities, and individuals, private or
> public, in the furthering of educational youth/adult sports.

## Divisions

All levels of play are available for men, women, and coed teams. Recreational leagues
are for non-competitive play; the lettered divisions are for teams that want a
tougher schedule.

| Division | Format | Games |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Men's Division I | Men's, 10+ players | 18 |
| Men's Division E | Men's, 10+ players | 18 |
| Men's Recreational | Men's, 10+ players | 12 |
| Women's Division | Women's, 10+ players | 16 |
| Coed Red / White / Blue | At least 5 men and 5 women | 12 |

Men's and women's teams usually play Monday and Tuesday with some Wednesdays. Coed
teams usually play Wednesday through Friday. Team fees are set based on the number of
games played.

## Season and registration

Teams register in person each spring. For the 2026 season, registration was held
Tuesday, April 14 from 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM in the meeting room of American Legion Post
2, at the corner of Custer and Villard Avenues in Helena. Enter the meeting room from
the door in the back parking lot.

Leagues begin around the first of May.

## Roster and player eligibility

A player must be **fifteen (15) years of age by December 31** to be eligible to play.
Any player under the age of 18 must have a signed permission form, or be signed for by
a parent or guardian on the official roster, before playing. Permission forms are
available at the concession stands at each field complex and on this site. The form is
kept on file with the rosters.

It is recommended that minor players wear a batting helmet at all times for concussion
prevention.

If your official roster has already been submitted and your team has changes, use the
change of roster form.

## Looking for a team, or looking for players?

Plenty of teams are short players and plenty of players want to get picked up. Contact
a board member below, or post in the league's player pick-up group on Facebook.

## Annual meetings

League business, fees, rules, and financial reports are covered at the annual meetings
each spring. One representative from each team is asked to attend.
