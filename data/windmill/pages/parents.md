## Game day
Games are played rain or shine unless you hear otherwise. Watch the homepage banner and your team's schedule for any rainouts or last-minute changes, and check the field page for directions to away games.

## Following your team
Every game's location links straight to driving directions. Standings and scores update on their own within about a minute of a coach reporting a final, so there is nothing to refresh or wait on.
