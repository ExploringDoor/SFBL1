# Rules

The full Windmill Fastpitch rulebook for the 2027 season is being finalized. In the meantime, here are the game formats, game-time rules, and the approved game balls by age group.

## Game formats by division

| Division | Night | Format | Scores kept |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8U Machine | Wednesday | Single game | No, blind-draw tournament seed |
| 8U Live | Wednesday | Single game, 90 min or 6 innings | Yes |
| 10U | Monday | Doubleheader | Yes |
| 12U | Tuesday | Doubleheader | Yes |
| 14U | Thursday | Doubleheader | Yes |
| High School | Wednesday | Single 7-inning game, no time limit | Yes |

## Game times and length

Doubleheaders play on Mondays (10U), Tuesdays (12U), and Thursdays (14U).

- The first game starts at 5:45 PM. The second game starts at 7:15 PM.
- No new inning begins after 65 minutes. Finish the inning already in progress.
- A 15-minute intermission between games gives families time to visit the concession stand.

## Game balls

Upon request, each team receives six league game balls for their age group. A team typically hosts four nights during the season. On the nights your team is the host, use the six balls this way:

- Night one: two new balls.
- Nights two, three, and four: one new ball and one good used ball.

An announcement goes out before the season when balls are available. Please request them and arrange pickup from the league coordinators.

Approved balls by age group:

- 12U and up: Dudley 12" ASA/USA leather softball, SB-12L #4A311Y
- 10U Live: Dudley 11" ASA/USA Fastpitch Softball, SBC11Y #4Y611
- 8U Live: Diamond 11" DRC-11RFPSC Reduced Compression Yellow FP softball
- 8U Machine Pitch: Diamond DFPM 12 Green Foam Dimple Ball, 12"
- 10U Machine Pitch: JUGS B2020 Sting Free Yellow Dimpled Softball, 11"

All live-play balls are stamped ASA/USA.
