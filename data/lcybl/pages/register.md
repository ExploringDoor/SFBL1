---
title: Register a Team
---

Clubs place teams with LCYBL each spring, and again for fall ball. This page is
the whole process in order, with every form you need.

Questions on any of it go to the League President — see the
[Contact page](/content/contact).

## At a glance

- **Who registers:** member clubs, not individual families. Players join through
  their local club, and the club enters teams with LCYBL.
- **Where it happens:** the April coaches meeting. Meetings are at Penn Medicine
  Park, 650 North Prince St, Lancaster PA 17603.
- **Rosters lock:** June 1st. After that date a roster cannot be changed.
- **Fall ball:** no rosters required, and both bat standards are allowed.
- **Age groups:** 8u, 10u, 12u and 14u, each split into sections.

## Step 1 — Check your players' ages

Age determines which group a player is eligible for, and it is the first thing
to settle because it decides how many teams you are entering.

Use the **LCYBL Age Chart** for the season you are registering for. The chart is
published a season ahead, so confirm you are reading the right year.

## Step 2 — Submit the Team and Coach Information Sheet

The **Field, Team and Coach Information Sheet** tells the league who is running
each team and which field that team calls home. The league builds the schedule
from it, so a wrong or missing field here shows up as a scheduling problem all
season.

There is also an **Information Sheet** spreadsheet version, which is the one
most clubs now use.

## Step 3 — Submit your roster

The **LCYBL Roster Form** is due at the April coaches meeting.

Two rules govern who can be on it:

- **Boundary Rule** — players are expected to come from your club's area. Read
  this before you build a roster, not after.
- **Double Roster Rule** — governs when a player may appear on more than one
  roster.

Remember that **rosters lock on June 1st**.

## Step 4 — Certificates

- **Child Protection Policy Certificate** — required for coaches.
- **Catchers NOCSAE Chest Protector** — the equipment standard catchers must meet.

## Step 5 — Read the rules for your age group

- [Spring rules, 8u and 10u](/content/rules-8u-10u)
- [Spring rules, 12u and 14u](/content/rules-12u-14u)
- [Fall ball rules](/content/rules-fall)

Pitch counts are tracked all season. The league publishes a **Pitch Count Log**
and a **Pitch Tracker Log** for that.

## Where to find every form

All of the documents named above live on the
[League Documents](/content/documents) page, split into spring and fall.

## Key dates

The **LCYBL Calendar** and the **meeting dates** list set the season. The
**End of Season Dates** document sets when play finishes and the playoff and
year-end tournaments begin.

Meetings are held at **Penn Medicine Park, 650 North Prince St, Lancaster PA
17603**.

## Fall ball

Fall ball runs on a lighter footing:

- No rosters are required.
- Both bat standards are allowed, unlike spring.
- See the [Fall Ball page](/content/fall-ball) for dates, fees and format.
