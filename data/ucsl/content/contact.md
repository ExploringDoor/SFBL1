# Contact Us

**United Coed Softball League (UCSL)** — adult 21+ coed recreational softball in Hamden, CT. Established 2016.

## Get in touch

- **Email:** untiedcoedsports@gmail.com
- **YouTube:** [United Coed Sports & Recreational Leagues](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCjY58mfii3TonS0FpBi8bHw)

## Where we play

Games are played at fields around Hamden, CT:

- Hamden Middle School
- Hamden High School
- Eli Whitney
- Dunbar Hill

## Join the league

Ready to play? [Register your team](/team-registration) for the season. Questions about divisions, the schedule, or getting a team together? Email us anytime — we'd love to have you.
