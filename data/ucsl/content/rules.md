_Coed slowpitch softball for adult recreational players. Based on the 2024 Spring/Summer umpire rules; confirm any updates with the commissioner before the season._

**EACH PLAYER MUST BE 21 YEARS OF AGE TO COMPETE IN THE LEAGUE.**

The league supplies each team with the game ball.

## Team Composition & Lineup

1. A team shall consist of **10 players** on the field made up of **6 males and 4 females**. There can never be more than 6 males on the field, but there can be more than 5 females. All players must have signed the Waiver Form before they can play.
   - **Lineup with 4 females:** Female, Male, Female, Male, Female, Male, Female, Male, Male, Male.

2. There must be **9 players to start** a game (3 must be female, with the auto-out at the top of the lineup). If this is not accomplished, it is a forfeit. The leadoff (Female) takes an auto-out.
   - **Lineup with 3 females:** Female (OUT – not available), Male, Female, Male, Female, Male, Female, Male, Male, Male.

3. **(SUB RULE)** Only if a team is short players. A team may only sub a player from another roster to be eligible to play.
   - a. Short at 9 players → may sub one **Female** from another roster to make 10.
   - b. Short at 8 players (must already have at least 3 females) → may sub one **Male** from another roster to make 9. May **not** pick up 2 males to make 10.
   - c. No team may pick up 2 players from another roster to make 10.
   - d. May not pick up a player if your roster already has 10 rostered players.

4. Batting order alternates male-female or female-male. Exceptions: playing with more women, or fewer than 10 players. Two females may bat back-to-back; two males may not. The **top 8 batters must be alternated**, and a **female must lead off**.

5. If a man pitches, a woman must catch, and vice versa. There must always be at least one female and one male player in the outfield (even with only 9 players / 3 females).

## Playoff Eligibility & Lineup Cards

6. Players must play in **at least 6 regular-season games** on their rostered team to be playoff eligible.
   - a. Lineup cards must be given to the umpires before the start of the game.
   - b. Only players who played in the game should be listed on the lineup card.
     - Lineup cards must be accurate and legible — first name or initial and last name only. **No nicknames.**
     - Mark a sub / fill-in with an asterisk (*). Examples: "Joe Smith", "J. Smith", "Joe S."
   - **Required on the lineup card** (or it will not be accepted): Date, Team Name, Opponent Team Name, Players in Lineup.

## Run Rule & Baserunning

7. **Run rule:** 20 runs after 3 innings, 15 after 4 innings, 10 after 5 innings. The home team shall bat.

8. No stealing bases and no leading off — a runner may not leave the base until the batter contacts the ball.

9. **Courtesy runners** are allowed. They must be declared before the game (unless an injury occurs during the game). The last batter out is the courtesy runner. Only 2 runners per game. A male runs for a male and a female for a female.

## Extended Lineup, Equipment & Pitching

10. A team may bat a maximum of **12** — only if 5 or 6 females are in the lineup.

11. **No metal cleats.** Plastic or molded only.

12. The league supplies each team with a core .40 restricted-flight ball: **Baden USSSA Classic M Slow Pitch 12" Softball**.

13. **All pitch counts start at 1 and 1.** Courtesy fouls are not allowed.

14. **Extended home-plate rule** is in effect:
    - **Strike zone:** an extended home plate whose width is the normal plate width and whose depth extends ~2.5 ft behind the front of the plate. Any pitch landing partially or wholly in this area is a strike.
    - **Arch:** 10 ft high / 6 ft low. If the ball hits any part of home plate, it's a strike. A runner must tag home plate to score — not the mat.

15. All bats (wood) are provided by the team. **100% wood bats only — no composite bats.** A bat list is provided to team captains and is available on the league website.

16. The DeMarini Corndog(s) and DeMarini Steel(s) single-wall softball bats are for **female use only**; grips and league stickers are provided by the league.

## Umpires, Conduct & Game Play

17. Umpire fee is **$70.00 per game ($35 per team)**, 2 umpires per game.

18. Only the team captain may question an umpire's call on the field.

19. If a player is ejected: 1st offense is a 1-game suspension; 2nd offense is removal from the league.

20. If a **male is walked with 2 outs**, he gets 2 bases — only if a female bats next. The next-up female then has the option to walk or hit. All forced runners advance on the walk.

21. The runner must tag the **outside (orange) base at 1st**. On a hit to the outfield, the runner/batter may tag either bag.

22. Both teams must confirm the score with the umpires between innings.

23. When a female is at bat, outfielders must remain **40 ft from the infield** (lines/cones will mark the field).

24. No cursing or confrontation with the umpire or opposing team. Fighting or abusive conduct results in immediate ejection; ejected players must leave the field immediately.

## Forfeits, Scoring & Season

25. **Forfeits:**
    - a. A team that forfeits after the deadline pays both teams' umpire fees ($70).
    - b. A forfeit is official only if that week's games are played as scheduled.
    - c. Teams have until **3 pm Wednesday** to declare a forfeit without the $70 penalty.

26. The final score must be texted or emailed to the league for confirmation.

27. **14-game season.** A player must play in at least 6 games to be playoff eligible.

28. **Game length:** 1 hr 15 min maximum. No new inning after 1 hr 10 min; the home team does not bat if ahead at the time limit. No grace period.

29. **Captain responsibilities:** notify team members of all rules and ensure they are observed and that players meet eligibility; have the team ready at game time; complete lineup cards correctly; the winning captain texts the league the final score.

30. No team may play with 5 defensive players on the infield.

31. **Reminder:** drinking alcoholic beverages is prohibited at the school fields (Hamden Middle, Hamden High, Eli Whitney, Dunbar Hill). A team caught drinking in the field of play receives a warning; a repeat results in ejection.
