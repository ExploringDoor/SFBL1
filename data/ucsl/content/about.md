# About United Coed Softball

United Coed Softball League (UCSL) is the adult softball program of **United Coed Sports & Recreation Leagues**, a Hamden, CT organization that also runs coed volleyball, kickball, and soccer for adults. Established in 2016, UCSL offers coed recreational softball for players of all skill levels — a fun, family atmosphere for adults to compete.

## Our Philosophy

United Coed Sports & Recreation Leagues provides leagues for adult players of all skill levels to enjoy, in a fun and family atmosphere for adults to compete. We strive to establish a safe and caring environment that allows for individual and social growth by providing and facilitating positive fun with responsible leadership.

## Sportsmanship Vow

> I believe in sportsmanship first. The sport is the game, and we are all in it together. While I am very competitive, I also want to support all the competitors as we strive to make our league great.

## The Basics

- **Ages 21+** — every player must be 21 or older to compete
- **Coed slowpitch softball** — 10 players on the field (6 men, 4 women), a 14-game season
- **Three divisions** — Gold, Silver, and Bronze, by skill level
- **Where we play** — fields around Hamden, CT: Hamden Middle, Hamden High, Eli Whitney, and Dunbar Hill
