## Purpose

The East Texas Basketball League exists to prepare kids for middle-school basketball. Coaches are expected to teach fundamentals, give every player meaningful minutes, and model sportsmanship for their team and the stands.

## Game format

| | 3rd Grade | 4th Grade | 5th / 6th Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Periods | 4 × 8 min | 4 × 8 min | 4 × 8 min |
| Clock | Running; stops last 2 min of the 4th | Running; stops last 2 min of the 4th | Stops on whistles in the 4th |
| Ball | 27.5" | 28.5" | 28.5" |
| Rim height | 8'6" | 10' | 10' |
| Free-throw line | 12' | 15' | 15' |
| Overtime | None (tie stands) | One 2-min period | One 2-min period |

## Playing time

Every player on the roster who is present and eligible must play in each half. Coaches, not the league, are responsible for tracking this.

## Defense

- **3rd and 4th grade:** man-to-man only. No zone, no press, no double-teams outside the lane.
- **5th / 6th grade:** man-to-man or zone. Full-court press allowed only in the 4th period, and never when leading by 15 or more.

## Conduct

- One coach may stand; assistants stay seated.
- A technical foul on a coach is an automatic seat for the rest of the game. A second in a season is a one-game suspension.
- Parents and fans who are asked to leave a gym by a commissioner must leave. Repeat problems are handled by the league.

## Scores and standings

The home town's commissioner enters the final score on this site after each game. Standings are by winning percentage; ties are broken by point differential.

## Rescheduling

Weather and gym closures are announced on the home page. Make-up games are scheduled by the two towns' commissioners.
