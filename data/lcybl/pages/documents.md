## Pitching and safety

| Document | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| LCYBL Pitch Count Log (v02.15.24v1) | The official log sheet that must be at every game. Records date, pitches or innings, pitcher name, uniform number, both managers' signatures, whether rest is required and the date the pitcher is eligible again. Carries the rest table and the limits: 8u = 50, 10u = 65 or 9 outs, 12u = 85, 14u = 95. Penalty for a violation is forfeit, a one game suspension and a $50 fine. |
| LCYBL Pitch Tracker Log (v04.05.23) | The in-game pitch-by-pitch tracker, with the pitch rules and the marking convention for balls, strikes and end of half-inning. Alert the team manager 10 pitches out from each threshold. |
| Catchers NOCSAE Chest Protector (3.24.21) | Reference document on the NOCSAE chest protector requirement. |
| Baseball wristband usage confirmation memo (4.4.23) | NFHS memorandum from B. Elliot Hopkins confirming that play-calling wristbands must be worn on the wrist, not the belt, under NFHS Baseball Rule 1-5-11. |
| Bat Decertificaion (3.10.20) | Bat decertification reference. See also the USA Bat decertified bats list. |
