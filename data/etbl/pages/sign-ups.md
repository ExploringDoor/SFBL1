## What to expect

- **Grades:** 3rd through 6th, with boys' and girls' divisions.
- **Deadlines are firm.** Programs cannot add players after their deadline, because teams are formed and the league schedule is built from those numbers.
- **Evaluations** (where a program holds them) are how players are placed on balanced teams — they are not tryouts, and every registered player is placed.
- **Season:** Saturday games in local gyms. See the [Schedule](/schedule) once it's posted, and the [Gyms](/fields) page for directions.
