## Lancaster County Youth Baseball League, Spring Rules Summary

**National Federation of HS Rules Apply unless League specific (not PIAA). Updated 03/10/26.**

Rule reference numbers are the league's own. Columns are 10U Section 1, 10U Section 2, 10U Section 3 (new 2.06.24), and 8U.

## At a glance

- **Game length:** 6 innings at every level on this sheet.
- **Mercy rule:** 15 runs after 4 innings, 10 runs after 5 innings (rule 6). 9 batters with a 10 run lead ends an inning (rule 7).
- **Pitch counts:** 8u = 50, 10u = 65 or 9 outs (rule 29).
- **.750 rule:** rule 53, applies to 10U Sections 1 and 2.
- **Tiebreakers:** points, head to head, runs allowed, coin flip (rule 37).

## 4. Regular Season Complete Game, due to darkness, rain, etc. (updated 04.17.24)

3 1/2 innings if the home team is leading and 4 innings if the away team is leading; games called prior to a complete game will be replayed in full. If the home team ties or leads at the bottom of the inning that will be the finial score of the game.

## 5. Game Ties

Games can end in a tie, but extra innings should be played as long as conditions permit as determined by the Umpire-in-charge.

## 6. Mercy Rule, game rule (updated 5.21.24)

15 runs after 4 Innings, 10 runs after 5 innings. If the home team is losing they must be given a chance to bat. If not the score reverts to the previous innings score.

## 7. Mercy Rule, inning

9 batters with 10 run lead.

## 8 and 9. Bats (updated 02/20/21)

100% Metal Bats size limitation. For 12u and below, the bats must be either 1.15 BPF (old standard) or USABAT standard (new standard). There is NO limitation on the bat diameter!

Barrel size and no weight/length drop limitation (ie -11, -10, -9, etc); The penalty for use of an illegal bat is the head coach is ejected and any related fine will be at the discretion of the league. All bats must have a Bat Performance Factor (BPF) of 1.15 or less or be USABAT certified. The list of approved bats is on the web site. No limitation on 100% wood bat.

## 16. Advancing / Stealing Home

**10U Section 1:** Batter may only advance home when forced by a walk, a hit batter, or a play started by a batted ball. Stealing home is not permitted. Upon a violation, the defense has the option to accept the outcome of a play or to send the runner back. If a catcher throws the ball in an attempt to throw out a runner in between pitches the runner at third can advance home. This rule will start on May 1st.

**10U Sections 2 and 3, and 8U:** Batter may only advance home when forced by a walk, a hit batter, or a play started by a batted ball. Stealing home is not permitted. Upon a violation, the defense has the option to accept the outcome of a play or to send the runner back.

## 17. Courtesy Runners for Pitcher and Catcher (updated 10.26.20)

Voluntary at any time; same runner cannot run for the pitcher and catcher during an inning; the runner must be a player who is not in the batting order at the time; if a team bats the entire line-up, the runner then must be the player that made the last out in the previous inning. If that last out of the previous inning is the pitcher or catcher of record*, then the next previous batter will be the courtesy runner. *If the batter has not pitched or caught in the game, they are not the pitcher or catcher of record and are not eligible to be ran for. (exception is the first inning)

## 22. Injured player's spot in hitting lineup and no replacement

If a team (except 8u and 10u Section 2) chooses to bat the roster in a game, an out is taken anytime a batter's spot is vacated in the line-up. This applies to injury, sickness, etc. No exceptions apply. For 8u and 10u Section 2, since the teams are required to bat the line-up, an out will not be taken for an injury or sickness, but an out in the batting spot will be taken for a player ejected from the game. For 8u or 10u Section 2, if the number of players batting falls below 9 players and out will be recorded for the players spot that was vacated to fall below 9 hitters.

## 29. Pitch Count / Rest / Re-entry (updated 02.06.24)

8u = 50, 10u = 65 or 9 outs. 1-25 pitches in a day = NO calendar day of rest, 26-35 pitches in a day = (1) calendar day of rest, 36-50 pitches in a day = (2) calendar days of rest, 51-65 pitches in a day = (3) calendar days of rest, 66+ pitches in a day = (4) calendar days of rest. Violation of Pitch count / rest; Forfeit / League fine. Once a pitcher is removed; the pitcher cannot return to pitch. *A pitcher may finish a batter but that will be the final pitch count. If there is a double header you can use the maximum daily pitch count. (Game 1 & Game 2 total pitch count for the day). A Catcher can also pitch with the same requirments as a Pitcher. Must use LCYBL pitch count log.

## 30. Ejections & Fines

Any player, manager, coach, fan, who is ejected from a game by an umpire will be penalized $50 and suspended for the next game (not allowed at game) on the 1st occurrence (players not fined); Penalties increase for additional occurrences; Fines are paid by the Organization of which the person ejected is a member; Organizations can decide whether to be reimbursed for the fine from the member who was penalized.

## 31. Infield Practice before game (updated 02.06.24)

Away team is provided the field for infield / outfield practice 15 minutes prior to the start of the game; Home team has the field prior to use by the away team.

## 42. Bringing Players up (updated 3.29.23)

You can bring up players from lower age / divisions or your own in-house leagues only if you dip below 9 (10 for 10u2).

Bringing up Pitchers 2+ rule. Example. 14u2 can not bring up a 14u3 player. Must be lower.

If you have two teams in the same division, you cannot share them in the same division! (no lateral moves) A player carried on the official League roster of any 10u Section 1 team may not pitch for any 12u Section 3 or 14u Section 3 team.

## 43. Batting Substitution

Once a substitution bats or runs for a player, the sub(s) and the original starter are locked into that batting position and either one may bat or run in that position ONLY.

## 44. Catcher or Any Fielder Obstruction

The catcher or any fielder can not block access by the runner to a base without possession of the ball. If this rule is violated, the umpire has the right to award the appropriate bases to the runner.

## 45. Offensive Visits

Each offensive team is granted 1 charged conference per inning to permit the coach or team's personnel to confer with base runners, the batter, on deck batter or other offensive team personnel; any requests more than 1 per inning will be denied by the umpire.

## 46. Intentional Walk

An intentional base on balls may be granted by the defensive team coach or catcher upon request to the umpire to award the base without throwing four balls; it can be requested upon any count on the batter.

## 47. Thunder & Lightning

When thunder is heard, or a cloud-to-ground lightning bolt is seen, suspend play and take shelter immediately. Thirty-minute rule: Once play has been suspended, wait at least 30 minutes after the last thunder is heard or flash of lightning is witnessed prior to resuming play.

## 48. Catch and Carry Rule

Each runner is awarded one base, if less than two outs a batter hits a fair or foul ball which is caught by a fielder, who then leaves the field of play unintentionally with both feet or by falling into a bench, dugout, stand, bleacher, or over any boundary or barrier such as a chalk line, fence, rope, or pre-game determined imaginary boundary line.

## 49. Jewelry (Updated 3.20.23)

As of the 2023 season, jewelry is now allowed to be worn by Players & Coaches. Umpire does have discretion to have the player remove jewelry if deemed unsafe or distracting.

## 50. 3rd to 1st pickoff

## 51. Playoff Game

If a playoff game is stopped due to weather and before it becomes official it will be continued the next work day (Monday ~ Friday). There are no regular or playoff games on the Friday / Monday of a holiday weekend unless both Coaches agree. Exception would be the LNP (series and tournament games).

## 52. Batting Helmet

No tape allowed on the helmet. Helmet cannot be distracting.

## 53. .750 Rule

**10U Sections 1 and 2:** Any club that does not enter a team in Section one will be ineligible to participate in the playoffs, year end tournament, or any division winner awards if those teams have a winning percentage of .750 or above. Only the team that is below .750 would be eligible.

**10U Section 3:** N/A. **8U:** N/A.

## 54. Relief Pitcher warmup

New pitcher or relief pitcher may have up to 8 warm-up pitches. Returning pitcher may have up to 5 pitches. For the relief pitcher, coach must be off the field before the 8th pitch is thrown. Penalty - 1st time warning, 2nd, charged mound visit.

## 55. Pitching Restrictions

"Plus One Rule". A player may not pitch in any game in a section which is greater than one section level below the section of the team carrying such player on its official League roster, regardless of age level. Example: A player carried on the official League roster of any 10u Section 1 team may not pitch for any 12u Section 3 or 14u Section 3 team.

## 57. Catcher's Helmet (updated 3.20.23)

The catcher's helmet and mask combination shall meet the NOCSAE standard. Any helmet or helmet and mask combination shall have full ear protection (dual ear flaps). A throat protector, which is either a part of or attached to the catcher's mask, is mandatory. A throat protector shall adequately cover the throat. The commercially manufactured catcher's head, face and throat protection may be a one-piece or multi-piece design. While in a crouch position, any non-adult warming up a pitcher at any location shall wear a head protector, a mask with a throat protector and a protective cup (male only).

Only manufacturer face shield is allowed and can only be clear. No aftermarket eye shields are allowed. No tinted face shields are allowed.

## 58. Catcher's Chest Protector

Effective for the LCYBL 2021 season, chest protectors used in LCYBL games be NOCSAE certified. The 2020 NOCSAE catcher's gear rule change will see that all high school and college level baseball players will be required to wear a chest protector that is NOCSAE certified for protection against Commotio Cordis. Every catcher's chest protector made prior to 2019 does not have the certification, so you will most likely need to get a new catcher's chest protector for the 2020 season. All NOCSAE certified chest protectors will have a stamp that shows it is certified by SEI and NOCSAE for protection against Commotio Cordis.

## 59. One-way electronic communication devices (added 2.06.24)

- Only from the dugout by one Coach to the catcher while on defense
- Coach cannot be outside the dugout/bench area
- PENALTY: The umpire shall issue a team warning to the coach of the team involved and the next offender(s) of that team will be ejected along with the head coach
- The interpretation of this rule is that the head coach and the offender in the dugout is ejected, not the player
- If one teams One-way communications fails the other team is still allowed to use theirs
- If a One-way communication fails and the catcher goes into the dugout to get it repaired is this considered a defensive visit, NO
- If a One-way communication fails and the coach comes out to visit the catcher to fix the device, is this considered a defensive visit, YES

## 60. Wristband (added 2.06.24)

- Single solid color

- Must have a flap to cover the white card

- If used must be on non-pitching hand
- PENALTY: The umpire shall issue a team warning to the coach of the team involved and the next offender(s) of that team will be ejected along with the head coach

## 61. Eye black (Updated 3.11.25)

If Eye Black is used it must be black and can not be wider than 1 1/2 inches and the thickness is not more than a finger width. It must be a straight line with no words or symbols. Penalty: Player must remove any Eye Black that does not follow the above proper use. There is no ejection. The player can not play untl the Eye Black is worn properly.

## 62. Resin, pine tar or drying agents are permitted to enhance the hold on the bat (2.18.25)

It is discovered that Player A (a) prior to entering the batter's box; (b) after entering the box; (c) after a base hit, is using a bat that has pine tar at 18 inches from the base of the knob. RULING: In (a), the illegal bat is removed from the game. In (b), the batter is declared out and the illegal bat is removed from the game and the coach is restricted to the dugout. In (c), the batter is declared out; the illegal bat is removed from the game and all base runners return to previous occupied base and the coach is restricted to the dugout.
