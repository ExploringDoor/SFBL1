# Forms and Documents

Everything a manager needs for the season. Links open the league's official files.

## Rosters and waivers

- [USA Softball Official Roster and Waiver](https://s3.amazonaws.com/my.llfiles.com/00062018/USA_Official_ROSTER_and_WAIVER.pdf)
  — the official roster every team files with the league.
- [Parental Consent Form](https://s3.amazonaws.com/my.llfiles.com/00062018/PARENTAL_CONSENT_FORM_2025.pdf)
  — required for any player under 18. Also available at the concession stands at each
  field complex.
- Change of roster form — file this when your official roster has already been
  submitted and your team has a change. Ask a board member for a copy.

## Equipment

- [Certified Equipment List](https://s3.amazonaws.com/my.llfiles.com/00062018/Certified_Equipment_HSA_Softball.pdf)
  — bats and equipment approved for HSA play.

## Schedule files

The live schedule on this site is the one to trust during the season; it reflects
makeups and postponements as they happen. These are the league's printed season files.

- [2026 Full Schedule (PDF)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/my.llfiles.com/00062018/Full%20Schedule%202026.pdf)
- [2026 Schedule (Excel)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/my.llfiles.com/00062018/2026%20HSA%20Schedule.xlsx)
- [2026 Key to Team Abbreviations (PDF)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/my.llfiles.com/00062018/KEY2AB26.pdf)
  — every team's abbreviation and its full sponsor name.

## A note on rules

Rules are provided to each team at registration. HSA plays under USA Softball rules as
adopted by the league. Questions about a rule or an interpretation go to your division
president or the Helena District Umpire In Chief; see the Contact page.
